DATA ENGINEERING

Better decisions start with better data.

A dashboard can show a number. Reliable data helps you understand what that number means.

LeeqTech Insights · Practical guide

Start with the decision

Identify the questions your team needs to answer. For example, which requests are taking longest to resolve, or where is a process slowing down? Work backwards from those decisions to the information needed.

Give important data an owner

Someone should be responsible for defining each important field, resolving discrepancies and maintaining quality. Agree on common definitions so that teams are not using the same label for different things.

Connect sources deliberately

Spreadsheets, business applications and operational systems often hold different parts of the picture. A pipeline can bring them together, but it needs clear rules for matching records, handling duplicates and responding to failures.

Build checks into the process

Look for missing values, unexpected changes, duplicate records and inconsistent formats. Make failed checks visible to the people who can investigate them. An automated process should not quietly turn unreliable inputs into confident-looking reports.

Keep access and maintenance in view

Limit access to what each person needs and document how information moves between systems. Plan for changes in source systems, reporting requirements and ownership. Data foundations need ongoing attention, not just an initial setup.

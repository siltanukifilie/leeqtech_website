What does LeeqTech do?+

We offer technology consulting, training, AI solutions, data engineering, IT solutions and automation. You can start with one service or discuss a project that connects several.

How do we start a project?+

Start by describing your goal, current challenge and preferred timeline. We can then discuss requirements, define the scope and agree on the next steps before implementation.

Can you work with our existing systems?+

Integration is part of our service offering. The approach depends on your systems, available interfaces and access requirements, which need to be assessed during discovery.

Do you provide training for non-technical teams?+

Yes. Training can cover practical AI use, data literacy and technology fundamentals. Topics and depth can be adapted to the experience of your team.

How much does a project cost?+

Pricing depends on scope, complexity, integrations and delivery requirements. A proposal can be prepared after these details are understood; there is no fixed price listed here.

How do you approach data privacy and security?+

Access needs, data sensitivity and appropriate safeguards should be agreed during planning. Please avoid sending passwords, confidential records or sensitive personal information through the enquiry form.

Can we start with a small project?+

Yes. A focused assessment, workshop or pilot can help clarify the opportunity before committing to a wider implementation.

Do you offer support after delivery?+

Discuss your handover, training and ongoing support needs during scoping so that responsibilities and any support arrangements are clearly agreed.
